Everything Bible

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Everything Bible is a free app for reading the Bible and keeping track of what you have read. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing about you, and there is nothing to buy.

Your reading and notes

Your reading progress, notes, highlights, trackers, settings and quiz scores are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they also sync through your own private iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. The developer cannot see them, and they are never sent to the developer. If you delete the app, they are deleted from that device; the copy in your iCloud stays until you remove it in your iCloud settings.

Backups

Save a Backup File makes a file with your notes, highlights, reading progress, trackers and settings. It goes only where you save or send it.

What the app does not do

There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or device identifiers, and it does not share any data with other companies.

Downloading a Bible

The app can download a Bible in your language. To do this it asks a public web server, a free GitHub Pages site run by the developer, for a text file. The request does not contain any personal information. Like every web server, GitHub may keep ordinary technical records such as your IP address, under GitHub's own privacy statement. The developer does not see those records. The app checks each download against a fingerprint before installing it.

Reporting a bug

The Report a Bug screen writes a report that you send yourself, through Mail, Messages or any app you choose. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you leave "Include app and device details" on, the report contains the app version, iOS version, device model, language and text size. It never includes your notes, highlights or reading history.

Prayer requests

The Prayer Request screen writes an email that you send yourself from Mail. It contains what you type, and your name unless you choose to stay anonymous. Mail also shows the address you send from. Requests are used only to pray for you, kept private, and never shared. Nothing is kept in the app.

Widgets

The Verse of the Day widget reads its settings from your device. The Lock Screen widget shows today's devotion title, which the app saves on your device for it. Neither sends anything anywhere.

Music

The music button opens a playlist in Apple Music. The app shares nothing with it.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
