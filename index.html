What it does

Everything for reading, in one place

Every part of the Bible has its own color, every page has a little life in it, and nothing is ever lost by missing a day.

Read and track

Tick off chapters as you read and watch every book fill in. Celebrate each chapter and every book you finish.

Notes and highlights

Write on any chapter or verse, highlight in six colors, and see every note on a colorful board.

A devotional each morning

A short devotional built on a verse, plus a Verse of the Day and an encouraging word for every part of the day.

Games and milestones

Trivia, Finish the Verse, a quiz on the books, fun facts, a Daily Challenge, and gold medals for what you finish.

Your language

Bibles in 14 languages to read offline, and an app that speaks 15. Every Bible is free and openly licensed.

Can we pray for you?

Send a prayer request privately, with your name or without it. Only the team reads it, and we will pray for you.

A look inside

Beautiful in light and dark

Drawn scenes, warm paper, and the serif the Bible reads in. Swipe through a few screens.
