Everything Bible

Read the Bible. Keep going.

A free iPhone app to read the Bible and keep track of what you have read. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and nothing to buy.

What it does

  • Tick off chapters as you read, and watch every book fill in. There are no streaks to lose.
  • Notes on any chapter or verse, and highlights in six colors.
  • Type John 3, 1 cor 13 or ps23 to go straight to a chapter.
  • A daily devotional, trivia, quizzes and fun facts.
  • Bibles in 14 languages: three English ones inside the app and 13 more you download once and read offline. The app itself speaks 15 languages.
  • A Verse of the Day widget in the colors you choose.
  • Light and dark mode, large text, and VoiceOver.
