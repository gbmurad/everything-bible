Everything Bible

Support

Everything Bible is a free app for reading the Bible and keeping track of what you have read, with notes, highlights, a daily devotional, trivia and Bibles in many languages. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Send a bug or an idea

In the app, open Settings and tap Report a Bug. Choose Bug, Idea or Other, write what happened, and send it through Mail, Messages or any app you like. Or write to escentialsllc20@gmail.com. Every message is read.

Questions people ask

How do I read the Bible in my language? Open Settings, then Bible version, and tap the download button next to a Bible. Each is about 1.3 megabytes and works offline once it is on your phone. On the Read tab a card also offers the Bible for the language your app is set to.

Which Bibles are there? The King James Version, American Standard Version and World English Bible are built in. Thirteen more download on request. The source and license of every one are listed in the app under Settings, About, and on the credits page.

Does it sync between my devices? Not yet. Your progress, notes and highlights live on your iPhone only, so they go if you delete the app.

Are there streaks? No. Milestones celebrate finishing things, and nothing is lost by missing a day.

How do I add the widget? Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap the plus button, choose Everything Bible, then pick a size. To change its colors and fonts, open Settings in the app.

Does it cost anything? No. Everything Bible is free, with no ads and nothing to buy.
